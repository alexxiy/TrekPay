TrekPay is a self-custodial Kerrigan wallet that keeps you in control. Your recovery phrase and keys stay on your device. There's no account to create, and nobody else can move your coins. TrekPay never holds your coins, and it doesn't exchange crypto or handle cash or bank payments.

Privacy
• Shielded transactions: shield, unshield and send KRGN privately, with an optional encrypted memo.
• Optional Tor or Nym for every connection.
• No ads and no tracking.

Fast payments, masternodes and governance
• InstantSend: payments locked by the masternode network are final within seconds.
• Masternodes and BroodNodes: register them from the wallet and follow their status and rewards.
• Governance: browse proposals, vote with your masternodes and submit your own.

Kerrigan NameSpace
• Register an easy name like yourname.kerrigan (or .krgn, .private, .secure, .safe) and share it instead of a long address.
• Point your name to any shielded address, yours or someone else's, and change it whenever you like. People keep paying the same name.
• Give a name to someone else, or list it on the Kerrigan NameSpace marketplace, where you can also find names from others.
• Your names belong to your recovery phrase, so the same phrase opens them on kerrigan-name.space too.
• Pay to a KNS name instead of an address. TrekPay checks where the name points before you send.
• KNS names are run by Kerrigan NameSpace, a service separate from this wallet.

Everyday use
• Scan QR codes and open kerrigan: payment links.
• Address book and coin control.
• Sign and verify messages.
• A clear history: masternode rewards, proposal payments and shielded transactions are labelled, and you can filter by each kind.
• Several accounts from one recovery phrase, and view-only wallets from an extended public key or a shield viewing key.
• Available in 15 languages. TrekPay follows your phone's language, or you can pick one in Settings.

Backups and security
• Restore with your 12- or 24-word recovery phrase, with an optional passphrase.
• Encrypted backups of your wallets and settings, plus automatic backups.
• PIN and biometric lock, and a duress PIN that opens the app with only the wallets you choose.

Your own server, if you like
TrekPay connects to its own bridge servers in Europe, the US and Singapore by default and uses the fastest one. You can switch to your own kerrigan-bridge server at any time.

Keep your recovery phrase written down somewhere safe. Whoever has it controls your coins, and nobody, the TrekPay team included, can restore it for you.
